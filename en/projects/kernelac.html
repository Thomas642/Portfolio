IT overhaul for a multi-site town hall

The year-long project of the ASR bachelor's degree: model, deploy, then maintain and secure the IT system of a fictional town hall with 79 staff across 12 sites, acting as its IT manager.

Context

A case study provided by the school, starting from a harsh audit.

Kernelac town hall is a fictional local authority: 79 staff across 12 sites, including the town hall, the municipal workshops and ten remote sites (halls, cultural and sports venues, childcare). An audit by the consultancy IT Conseils sets the starting point.

  • ObsolescenceVMware ESXi 6.5 virtualisation, servers on unsupported Windows Server 2008 R2 and 2012 R2, workstations out of warranty.
  • Flat networkNo VLAN segmentation, guest network not isolated from the internal network.
  • SecurityNo MFA on remote access, no central monitoring or SIEM, untested backups.
  • ComplianceNo DPO, no record of processing activities, no DPIA; no security policy, no business continuity or disaster recovery plan.

Targets set: 99.9% IT availability, NIS2, ANSSI and GDPR compliance, tested backups, secure remote access and an eco-responsible approach.

Three blocks

Each block builds on the choices of the previous one.

  1. Block 1

    Model the infrastructure

    Analysis of the existing system and compliance audit, stakeholders, WBS and RACI matrix, specifications (technical functions and constraints), technology watch and reasoned choices, risk matrix (probability × severity), schedule, KPIs and CAPEX / OPEX budget.

  2. Block 2

    Deploy the infrastructure

    Deployment plan in 8 work packages with RACI and Gantt, LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi, virtualisation and Active Directory designs, flow matrix, Packet Tracer lab, plan of 63 unit tests, acceptance and budget tracking.

  3. Block 3

    Maintain and secure

    Remaining gaps after deployment ranked by criticality, corrective action plan, business continuity and disaster recovery plans, preventive and corrective maintenance, day-to-day administration and information security policy.

Target architecture

Two production sites backing each other up, ten remote sites connected over VPN.

Technical choices

As described in the Deploy block report.

AreaChosen solution
Network7 VLANs (administration, management, production, office, staff Wi-Fi, guest Wi-Fi, monitoring), 10.SITE.VLAN.HOST/24 addressing scheme, inter-VLAN traffic filtered by the firewall with deny by default
WAN1 Gbit/s FTTE fibre and 4G/LTE backup at both main sites, SD-WAN failover in under 30 s, IPsec AES-256-GCM / SHA-384 tunnel between sites
Remote accessRemote sites over IPsec VPN with X.509 certificates (AD CS PKI), mobile staff over SSL/TLS VPN with mandatory MFA
Wi-Fi15 Wi-Fi 6 access points, staff on WPA3-Enterprise (802.1X via NPS RADIUS), isolated guests with a captive portal
VirtualisationHyper-V on two sites, 8 VMs on the main host, Hyper-V Replica: 5-minute RPO, site failover in under 4 h
DirectoryActive Directory on Windows Server 2025, tiered admin accounts (T0, T1, T2), hardening GPOs (passwords, BitLocker, USB, session lock)
Backup3-2-1-1-0 strategy with Veeam: immutable local NAS, copy at the workshops, encrypted copy in a SecNumCloud sovereign cloud, monthly restore tests
MonitoringZabbix for monitoring, Wazuh as SIEM with 12 months of log retention
Workstations79 EPEAT Gold certified laptops, BitLocker encryption, European EDR, Microsoft 365 Business Premium

Lab and tests

What was demonstrated, and what still has to be validated on real hardware.

Packet Tracer lab

Three representative sites (town hall, workshops, one remote site). Validated in the lab: inter-VLAN routing with 802.1Q sub-interfaces, WAN convergence through a simulated cloud, end-to-end connectivity between the two firewalls. IPsec, SD-WAN failover, 802.1X and ACL filtering require a licence not available in the lab: their configuration is documented for acceptance on the physical equipment.

Test plan and acceptance

63 unit tests in 8 categories: delivery, network, systems, workstations, backup, security, training and non-regression. They come before technical then functional acceptance, co-signed by the mayor and the chief executive.

Maintain and secure

Keeping the architecture running day to day and preparing for a crisis.

Remaining gaps

Gaps ranked by criticality (severity × probability), then handled over three horizons: 0 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, 6 to 12 months. An open-source ITSM/CMDB tool (GLPI) handles ticketing, inventory and the knowledge base.

Continuity and recovery

Targets: RTO under 4 h and RPO under 1 h. Five recovery sheets per service × risk pair, up to the total loss of the town hall site, with a restart order from the network up to the applications.

Maintenance

Incidents prioritised from P1 (under 30 min, on call) to P4 (under 5 days), following ITIL v4 good practice.

Security policy

Rights assigned with the AGDLP method, separate privileged accounts, incident procedure with notification of the CNIL (French data protection authority) within 72 h in case of a data breach, crisis contact list.
