Network switching overhaul at an industrial site

The nine distribution switches of a sawmill replaced with Aruba Instant On 1930 units, without stopping production, and fully integrated into monitoring.

PROJECT CARDLEON-SW

Company
Scieries Lesbats · Léon, France
Period
10/2025 – 04/2026
Role
Survey, pre-configuration, deployment, monitoring
Hardware
4 × JL681A · 5 × JL683B
Tracking
GestSup ticketing, RACI, Gantt

Context

The company processes maritime pine from the Landes forest across five sites, linked by VPNs managed by Stormshield firewalls. The Léon site, head office and production site, is segmented into 12 active VLANs: office, production, Wi-Fi, VoIP, management, storage, DMZ, inter-site federation and WAN links.

Its switching relied on switches from several vendors (Cisco, Dell and others), with four problems:

  • AgeingDegraded performance and a risk of failure, with a direct impact on production.
  • Mixed vendorsA different interface for each vendor, so longer diagnostics and more room for error.
  • UnmanagedSome switches had neither SNMP nor VLANs: segmentation could not be applied everywhere.
  • Blind spotsRecurring disconnections, hard to diagnose without visibility on every device.
Thesis question How can the Léon site's switching infrastructure be renewed and standardised to ensure the reliability, manageability and observability of the local network, while keeping the business running?

Technical choice

Standardise on the Aruba fleet already in place at another company site.

The chosen model is the Aruba Instant On 1930, already used at the Saint-Perdon site. After a full port inventory, a quote was requested and a price comparison was approved by management.

  • Central management through the Instant On cloud portal
  • 802.1Q VLANs, LACP, STP / RSTP
  • Class 4 PoE+ for Wi-Fi access points, cameras and IP phones
  • SNMP v1 / v2c / v3 for Zabbix monitoring
  • SFP / SFP+ ports for inter-switch links

Approach

Prepare as much as possible upfront to keep on-site work to a short window.

  1. 10/10/2025

    Survey of the existing network

    Project handed over. Port-by-port inventory in a tracking file, then devices placed on the Zabbix map on 23/10.

  2. 11/2025

    Budget and order

    Inventory completed on 07/11, quote requested, price comparison approved by management. Hardware received on 28/11.

  3. 12/12/2025

    Pre-configuration

    Switches added to the Instant On portal, naming convention, VLANs created, management addresses. Rules added on the Stormshield firewall and connectivity checked.

  4. 16/01/2026

    Phase 1: main fleet cut-over

    Replacement on a Friday afternoon while production was stopped, then on site the following Monday to fix remaining issues. Two missing switches identified.

  5. 20/02/2026

    Phase 2: canteen and extension

    The two additional switches (8 and 24 ports) installed, VLANs configured, connectivity validated.

  6. 03/04/2026

    Validation and close-out

    Final connectivity tests, inter-site VPN tests, integration into Zabbix and Instant On, technical documentation updated. Ticket closed.

Challenges

Port naming

Ports are not named the same way on Cisco (Fa0/1) and Aruba (GE1/0/1). When moving cables over, this caused a few VLAN assignment mistakes, fixed the Monday after the migration.

Two missing switches

Phase 1 revealed two locations not covered by the first order. An additional quote was approved and both switches were installed in a second phase.

Risks identified from the outset: service interruption, VLAN or addressing mistakes, PoE compatibility with access points, cameras and IP phones, cabling errors between vendors.
