Work · Bachelor thesis

Network switching overhaul at an industrial site

Scieries Lesbats · Léon site (40) · 10/2025 – 04/2026

The site ran ageing switches from several vendors (Cisco, Dell…), some of them unmanaged: no SNMP, no VLANs, so monitoring was incomplete and disconnections were hard to diagnose. I led the hands-on replacement of the nine distribution switches with Aruba Instant On 1930 units, matching the fleet at another company site.

  • Survey of the existing network: port inventory, devices placed on the Zabbix map
  • Pre-configuration in the Instant On portal: naming convention, VLANs, management IPs
  • Required flows opened on the Stormshield firewall before going on site
  • Physical cut-over while production was stopped, with fixes the following Monday
  • New switches added to Zabbix and Instant On monitoring

Lesson learned Port naming differs between Cisco (Fa0/1) and Aruba (GE1/0/1): that caused a few VLAN assignment mistakes when moving cables over.

  • 802.1Q
